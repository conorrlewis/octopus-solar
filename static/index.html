How you use energy

What solar could do for you

Solar output is a model: your annual estimate is spread over the year using a typical UK seasonal curve and sun position — real days vary with cloud. EV charging is detected from sustained high half-hourly draw; the Home / EV / Both filter chooses which of it the charts and the solar model cover (solar only offsets what is selected — Home by default, since the EV keeps charging off-peak). Costs use Octopus unit rates and standing charges when available.

Connect to Octopus Energy

Find your API key and account number at octopus.energy → Account → Personal details → API access. They are stored only in config.json on this computer and sent only to Octopus.
